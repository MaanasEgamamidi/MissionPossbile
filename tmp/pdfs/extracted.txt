Engine and scene

installed executable.
Authority and actions
GameServices is a scene-owned node, not an autoload. SessionService advances fixed ticks; ActionService handles
requests. NetSession supports up to four ENet peers with host authority.
Crew and access
CharacterState defines the four specialty IDs used on page 3. Existing crew switching and ownership must be reused.
Parked characters currently freeze personal condition/supply use.
Timing and recovery
SessionService.advance takes real_delta, then converts ticks to game hours for ColonySim. Current no-air health loss is 25
points per game hour; zero health invokes recovery. A real-second lethal timer needs deliberate integration.
Pressure and zones
GameServices currently derives outside from ColonyZones.EXTERIOR. Garden dome is treated as enclosed. A failed local
airlock needs a pressure/protection query beyond lighting-zone identity.
UI and content
UiPanel/UiRoot supply modal behavior and visible world margins. UiTheme and AstraStyle define navy/slate glass,
orange accents and teal focus. SourceLibrary and existing source/credit UI can support NASA provenance.
World and Blender
StationCatalog supports additive data/stations_*.json records. Colony materials are generated from palette/preset data.
Export scripts reference historical G: paths; the colony source .blend was not found in the workspace assets_src listing.
Verification
tools/run_tests.ps1 discovers specific Godot download paths. Existing suites cover saves, session, life support, UI and
LAN. F01 must discover actual tool paths and establish fresh results.

Power Room / N02
Stabilize the three numbered distributor channels by activating each ring in its alignment window. Any crewmember can
calibrate them. The electrical and communications specialist handles the circuit board: place and rotate conductors to reach
the output, then balance actual and target voltage. A connected wire path and the correct voltage are both required. Watch
local equipment condition as the colony continues to wear.
Indoor Greenhouse / N03
The biologist restores the plant-control profile by connecting attribute tokens and matching the displayed target counts.
Any crewmember can water the plant bed by adjusting guides to route the stream into it. Check the moisture gauge and the
repaired plant-control display. This is the indoor Greenhouse; the separate Garden dome has its own irrigation and glass
tasks.
Garden dome / N04
Switch to the field engineer to reconnect the irrigation route from pump to planter. Match pipe ports and watch water pass
through the solved path. Any crewmember can assemble the loose replacement-glass pieces into the square frame. The
pane must be complete, with no overlaps or gaps. After both repairs, the room reveal should show moving water and an
intact glass surface.

the self-test. Connected, powered and verified are separate cues. Read task instructions before pouring; these puzzle
reagents are not real medical treatment instructions.
Hangar / N06
Keep the suit-staging and entry surfaces clean by removing the marked dust patches; any crewmember can help. Switch to
the field engineer for the airlock seal: find the leak, seat the patch, tighten the indicated fasteners and verify pressure holds.
