# Mars ores and resources (research, 2026-11-14)

For the scout drone's mineral reports, the "scan for an ore" upgrades and what the colony can build. Facts on what each
mineral is, where it was found and by which instrument come from NASA / PDS / USGS / peer-reviewed sources below
(mostly abstracts, press releases and summaries). Colony uses marked SPECULATIVE are engineering ideas, not things done
on Mars.

## Databases we can cite

| Source | URL | Use |
|---|---|---|
| MICA (Minerals Identified through CRISM Analysis): ~30 Mars minerals with type spectra and example places | https://crismtypespectra.rsl.wustl.edu (PDS MRO-M-CRISM-4-TYPESPEC-V1.0; Viviano-Beck et al. 2014, JGR Planets) | **The game's "Mars mineral database"** |
| PDS Geosciences Node (CRISM, CheMin, TES, OMEGA archives) | https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu | Primary archive |
| Mars Orbital Data Explorer | https://ode.rsl.wustl.edu/mars | Data search (too technical for kids) |
| Curiosity CheMin data | https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/msl/msl-m-chemin-4-rdr-v1/ | Gale crater mineral tables (Rampe et al. 2020) |
| MGS TES project | https://themis.asu.edu/projects/tes | Hematite, olivine maps |
| USGS Geologic Map of Mars, SIM 3292 (Tanaka et al. 2014) | https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3292 | Rock units and ages ("what ground is here") |
| SWIM buried-ice maps (Putzig et al.) | https://photojournal.jpl.nasa.gov/catalog/PIA26045 | Where water ice is |

No single official "Mars ore database" exists; MICA + Ehlmann & Edwards (2014) is the closest real one.

## Resources

| # | Mineral (kid name) | Formula | Found by | Where | How common | Colony use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hematite ("rust rock", "blueberries") | Fe₂O₃ | MGS TES (Christensen 2000); Opportunity Mössbauer | Meridiani Planum; Aram Chaos; Vera Rubin Ridge (Gale) | Regional | Iron/steel; oxygen given off when the iron is reduced |
| 2 | Magnetite ("magnet rock") | Fe₃O₄ | Curiosity CheMin | Gale sandstones; common in basalt | Widespread, low % | Iron (magnetic separation from sand) |
| 3 | Iron-nickel meteorites ("space metal") | Fe-Ni | Opportunity "Heat Shield Rock" (2005); Curiosity "Lebanon" (2014) | Meridiani; Gale | Rare, scattered | Ready-made metal, nickel |
| 4 | Olivine ("green gem rock") | (Mg,Fe)₂SiO₄ | TES, CRISM; Perseverance PIXL/SHERLOC | Nili Fossae; Jezero (Séítah) | Regional | Magnesium, iron; absorbs CO₂ |
| 5 | Basalt / regolith ("Mars dirt") | plagioclase + pyroxene + olivine + 20–50 % glassy material | All rovers, TES | Everywhere | Widespread | Bricks, radiation shielding, 3D-printed shells, basalt fibre (SPECULATIVE / simulant tests) |
| 6 | Plagioclase / anorthosite ("sparkle stone") | CaAl₂Si₂O₈–NaAlSi₃O₈ | CRISM (Carter & Poulet 2013, "possible"); CheMin | Southern highland crater rims; Gale | Feldspar widespread; anorthosite rare | Aluminium, calcium (SPECULATIVE refining) |
| 7 | Clays ("water clay") | Fe/Mg smectites | Mars Express OMEGA, CRISM; CheMin | Mawrth Vallis, Nili Fossae, Gale (Yellowknife Bay), Jezero delta | Regional | Water when heated; ceramics |
| 8 | Gypsum ("water crystal salt") | CaSO₄·2H₂O | OMEGA (Langevin 2005), CRISM; Curiosity veins | North polar dunes (Olympia Undae); Gale veins | Regional | Water (~21 % by mass); plaster; sulfur |
| 9 | Mg sulfates / jarosite ("yellow acid salt") | MgSO₄·H₂O / KFe₃(SO₄)₂(OH)₆ | OMEGA, CRISM; Opportunity | Valles Marineris; Meridiani; Mount Sharp | Regional | Sulfur, magnesium, water |
| 10 | Pure sulfur ("yellow crystals") | S | Curiosity (2024) | Gediz Vallis, Gale | **Rare, one site** | Sulfur concrete (SPECULATIVE) |
| 11 | Carbonates ("fizz rock") | MgCO₃, CaCO₃ | CRISM (Ehlmann 2008); Phoenix; Perseverance | Nili Fossae; Jezero | Regional / rare | CO₂, lime cement |
| 12 | Opaline silica / tridymite ("glass stone") | SiO₂ | Spirit (Home Plate, ~91 % silica); CheMin (Morris 2016) | Gusev; Gale (Buckskin); Valles Marineris | Regional / rare | Glass, solar cells, optics |
| 13 | Chloride salts ("salt patches") | NaCl etc. | Odyssey THEMIS (Osterloo 2008, ~200 sites) | Southern highlands | Regional | Chlorine, sodium |
| 14 | Perchlorates ("danger salt") | ClO₄⁻ salts | Phoenix WCL (0.4–0.6 % of soil, Hecht 2009); Curiosity SAM | Phoenix site; likely global | Widespread | **HAZARD** (thyroid); also an oxygen source |
| 15 | Water ice ("frozen water") | H₂O | SHARAD, Odyssey neutrons, HiRISE, Phoenix | Polar caps; buried mid-latitude ice (mostly poleward of ~40°) | Widespread at high latitudes | Water, oxygen, rocket fuel |
| 16 | Mars air | CO₂ 95.1 %, N₂ 2.59 %, Ar 1.94 % | Curiosity SAM | Everywhere | Everywhere | Oxygen (MOXIE: 122 g total, up to 12 g/h), methane fuel, buffer gases |

**Never found on Mars (leave out or label "scientists guess"):** rare earths, gold, platinum-group ores.
Hazards: perchlorates (thyroid, destroy organics), fine abrasive dust.
