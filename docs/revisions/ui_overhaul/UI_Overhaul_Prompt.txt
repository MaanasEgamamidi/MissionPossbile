Overhaul UI

Redesign the interface so a child can tell what is happening, what they need to do, and which
control to use. The current UI is too complicated for the intended audience. Prioritize a simpler,
more visual presentation with clearer actions and less reading during play.

Before redesigning, review the full UI and trace how each screen and its shared components are
built. Identify the screens, states, and decisions that are hardest to understand. Use the Current UI
screenshots to document the baseline.

Use Medium UI as a starting point for visual cues and feedback. Simplify those patterns further.
Use the sketches, reference images, and specific examples explicitly designated as Best UI to
determine the final direction. Apply the principles from those examples to the rest of the interface.

Best UI

Use Among Us task screens as the benchmark for simplicity and intuitive controls. Keep text brief, use
color and shape to signal what belongs together, make the next action obvious, and show the result directly
on the puzzle.

Adapt those patterns to MissionPossible's own colony equipment and puzzle rules. Some tasks already
draw on Among Us. Keep the ease of use and give each minigame its own visual identity.

Custom Minigame Titles and Concepts

Give each repair an action title that makes sense in the colony. Make the equipment itself the interface.
Repeat familiar conventions for focus, prompts, and success across the game, and give each apparatus its
own layout and visual response.

Lock the Relay

T01 / Comms | Among Us reference: Find Signal

Use the radio console as the play surface. Turn one oversized knob until the live waveform overlaps a ghost
trace, then watch a short data pulse travel to a relay icon. Keep hold and transfer feedback on the scope.

Patch the Relay

T02 / Wires | Among Us reference: Fix Wiring

Give the relay a central patch bay with four chunky plugs and shape-keyed sockets. Connected plugs physically
seat and light a path to the relay. Show the electrical specialist badge where the first plug would be if the player
needs to switch crew.

Sync the Turbine

T03 / Rings | Among Us reference: Calibrate Distributor

Build the timing task into one turbine face with three concentric rings. Animate the active ring only and show
each secured ring with a lock mark. Keep the wider timing window available.

Light the Power Bench

T04 / Circuits | Among Us reference: Divert Power

Build a route across a service board from the power jack to a real lamp. Light the connected path up to the first
gap. Use one small meter beside the lamp to show the required voltage and whether a booster or reducer is
needed.

Water the Kale

T06 / Water | Among Us reference: Open Waterways

Rotate thick pipe pieces between a visible water tank and a kale tray. Animate water up to the first break. Try a
shorter 4-by-3 starter board and show the kale perk up as soon as water arrives.

Custom Minigame Titles and Concepts

Glaze the Dome

T08 / Glass | Among Us reference: Assemble Artifact

Place five colored panes into a dome viewport. Preview the outline under the held piece. Let sunlight pass
through each fitted pane and add a single shimmer when the whole viewport locks in.

Sort the Lab Samples

T09 / Mixture | Among Us reference: Sort Samples

Make the test-tube rack the play area. Put a shape on every color, tilt the selected tube toward a valid
destination, and lift settled tubes upright. Try opening with the tubes already standing for a shorter first puzzle.

Revive the Med Bay

T10 / Equipment | Among Us reference: Fix Wiring

Show two real devices beside their sockets. Use distinctly shaped plugs and give each powered device its own
response, such as a pulse trace or scanner sweep. Let the player start a brief self-test on the machines.

Clear the Landing Lines

T11 / Dust | Among Us reference: Clean O2 Filter

Use the hangar floor as the board. Brush clumps loose and vacuum the loose dust directly. Reveal landing
markings under the cleaned patches, with fewer patches on the first run.

Seal the Airlock

T12 / Seal | Among Us reference: Monitor Tree

Use the visible vapor leak to guide the player to a damaged seam. Fit a shaped gasket, press three highlighted
points in order, then watch a pressure needle rise. The gauge is the reference; the gasket interaction is specific
to MissionPossible.

T05 Elements and T07 Pipes use the maintenance interface in the checked project. The ten concepts here cover the
standalone broken-part minigames.
